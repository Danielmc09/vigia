# ADR-017 · Only indicators cross between businesses, never incidents (2026-10-27, Angel)

**Context.** The knowledge base protects everyone, but no business may learn that another was attacked.
**Options.** Share anonymized cases; share only fingerprints and the impersonated brand.
**Decision.** Only fingerprints (simhash, pHash, domain, account) and the brand; aggregates only for the operator; a cross-tenant isolation test.
**Why.** It's the Safe Browsing/VirusTotal/ISAC (TLP) pattern and what privacy-by-design requires in front of the judges.
**What would invalidate it.** Nothing.
