# ADR-082 · `scam confirmed <id>`: the owner feeds campaigns with fingerprints, shared only above 2 businesses (2026-10-28, Claude Code)

**Context.** T-10 asks the owner to confirm a scam and feed `known_campaigns` (D-017: only fingerprints cross businesses). Risk: a fake business could poison the pool with fingerprints of legitimate messages, flagging them as scam everywhere else.

**Decision.** Owner-only command (i18n words es+en); takes the saved case text (decrypted with the business's key), computes fingerprints (simhash, domains, no clear text or phones) and calls definer function `vigia_confirm_campaign`, which adds `Keyring.hash(business_id)` to `known_campaigns.confirmed_by`. `fetch_known_campaigns(conn, country, business)` returns the seed set, campaigns that business confirmed, and campaigns confirmed by ≥ `SHARED_AFTER = 2` distinct businesses.

**Ceiling.** Two fake businesses (enrollment is free) are enough to poison it; no human review, no expiry.

**What would invalidate it.** A poisoning incident, or enough business volume to justify raising the threshold; then operator review before sharing.
