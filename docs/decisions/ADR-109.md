# ADR-109 · A scheme-less host exists if its TLD is in the vendored IANA list; word TLDs need a hyphen (2026-10-31, Angel + Claude Code)

**Context.** D-039 accepted scheme-less hosts only with a 2-letter TLD or one from `SCHEMELESS_TLDS`, a hand-written list: `dian-devolucion.tax` or `irs-refund.tax` produced no signal at all (c-0241, c-0242). Angel asked to replace it with the official list. Checked beforehand: IANA (version 2026103100) has 1436 TLDs and many are words (final, casa, ltda, sas, gratis, uno, tienda, pay, one, you); 95 words from the evals texts are TLDs, and `example` is not one (RFC 2606, used in 10 tests).

**Options.** (a) IANA alone: `informe.final/v2`, `S.A.S/Ltda`, and `la.casa` would become hosts, and with a brand in front would give `brand_off_domain`, which is HARD and condemns on its own. (b) a longer hand list. (c) IANA decides what exists; TLDs outside the short list need a hyphen in the first label. (d) like (c) but also accept if the first label is a pack brand.

**Decision.** (c). `src/vigia/domain/tlds-iana.txt`, a copy of https://data.iana.org/TLD/tlds-alpha-by-domain.txt downloaded Oct 31 with its version header; read once at import time, never over the network. Scheme-less: 2 letters, or `SCHEMELESS_TLDS` (kept: abused and not words; keeps `example`), or an IANA TLD with a hyphen in the label right before the TLD (the registrable one: `www.irs-refund.tax` and `portal.dian-devolucion.tax` get in, c-0244/c-0245; the first version looked at the first label and the reviewer caught it). Outside IANA is rejected (`pagos-dian.soporte`, `x.zzpago`). Sentence case still requires a path and a known label. With `https://` nothing changes: any TLD.

**Why.** The asymmetry (Angel): a hard false alarm on a legitimate message costs far more than missing a scam on `.final`, which nobody uses. The hyphen almost always separates an assembled domain (`pagos-dian`, `Chase-Secure`) from a word stuck to another. (d) was dropped: it would couple `domain/urls.py` to country packs (the signals layer), and the cases it would cover (`dian.support`) are already covered by the short list.

**Not done.** IDNs (`xn--…`, 151 entries): the scheme-less regex doesn't capture Unicode outside Greek/Cyrillic; with a scheme they already pass. The list doesn't auto-update: bumped by hand if a new TLD shows up in an incident.

**What would invalidate it.** A scheme-less scam on a word TLD with no hyphen (`dianpagos.tax`) isn't extracted; if it shows up, incident and the TLD moves to the short list. A legitimate with a hyphen and a word TLD (`algo-asi.final`) comes out as a host; with a brand in front it would be a hard false alarm → incident.
