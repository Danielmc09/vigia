# ADR-098 · Resolved URLs only absolve the legit guard; raw URLs still decide (2026-10-31, Claude Code)

**Context.** Real case: a legitimate Cruz Verde message with an `ma.sv` link ends up doubtful. `build.py` computes `has_unofficial_url` over raw URLs, so a shortener is flagged "unofficial" even when it lands on the official domain. Once a resolver is injected, `detector.py` would compute its `unofficial` flag over the resolved URL, and that flag feeds `credentials_request` (hard) and `authority_with_link` (medium).

**Options.** (a) one single resolved flag for everything. (b) two flags: raw for `pattern_signals`, resolved only for `fuse`'s legit guard (`_rule4`). (c) don't resolve at all.

**Decision.** (b). Security reason: cloaking — a shortener can 302 to the official domain when it sees the server's IP and to phishing when it sees the user. With (a), 3 hops ending at `bancolombia.com` would downgrade "asks for your password" from hard to light and erase `authority_with_link`. With (b) the resolved URL can only stop an analyst LEGIT ≥0.7 from being degraded to doubtful because of the link; it never lowers a hard signal or removes a medium one. QR links are never resolved (stay raw in both paths). `fusion.py` is unchanged.

**What would invalidate it.** Another consumer of the resolved URL appearing — it would have to justify why it can't downgrade a hard signal.

**Addendum (Oct 31, judge FAIL → incidents c-0216, c-0217).** `_shortener` was replacing the medium `unresolved_shortener` signal with the destination's signals entirely: "DIAN … Pay today at bit.ly" with 3 hops to `bancolombia.com` dropped from scam (2 mediums) to doubtful. Always keeping the medium breaks the goal (c-0213 would get stuck at rule 3, never legit).

**Decision.** `detector.py` restores the shortener's raw medium signal whenever any other signal (patterns, sale sequence, QR, address book, another link — anything not carrying `via` from unrolling) is medium or hard; with no other risk signal, the destination decides. Round 2 of the judge: the first version only looked at `pattern_signals` and sale-sequence scams lost their verdict (c-0217).

**Response to red-team** (3 hops to an official site): with any other risk signal present, nothing is absolved (hard and medium stay); with no risk text, the raw case already had at most one medium (doubtful), and the only gain is that an analyst LEGIT ≥0.7 isn't degraded.

**What would invalidate it.** A risk pattern `pattern_signals` misses that only travels inside the cloaked link; that becomes an incident.
