# ADR-069 · `referencias_precio` as unverified estimates, floor 0.4 (2026-10-27, Claude Code)

**Context.** specs/15 fixes "price < 40% of the pack's reference" but gives no table.

**Decision.** `CountryPack.price_references` (`PriceReference`: item, pattern, price, source), 8 in CO (French bulldog 3.5M, golden 2M, pug 2M, husky 1.8M, iPhone 15/16 Pro 4M, iPhone 13-16 2M, NMax 13M, Pulsar 9M COP) and 5 in US (French bulldog 2500, golden 1500, yorkie 1200, iPhone 15/16 Pro 700, iPhone 13-16 350 USD). `source` reads "Claude Code estimate, unverified": nobody checked real market prices. The quoted price is the largest amount in the first non-buyer turn with money; the reference is the first whose pattern appears in the text (hence "Pro" is checked before the plain model). `PRICE_FLOOR = 0.4` in `signals/sequence.py`, per specs/15.

**Why.** 0.4 leaves margin: with ±30% estimate error the floor is still far from a real price (a golden at 2.4M doesn't trigger).

**What would invalidate it.** Angel verifying prices and one changing by more than 50% (fix the figure and the `source`).
