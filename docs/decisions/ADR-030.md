# ADR-030 · Registrable domain via a short public-suffix list, no `tldextract` (2026-10-27, Claude Code)

**Context.** URL signals (T-06) need the registrable domain (`bancolombia.com.co`, not `com.co`) to compare against the country pack; `tldextract` isn't installed and `uv add` was denied (D-024); tests and evals can't hit the network (tldextract fetches the PSL on first use).
**Options.** `tldextract`; a full vendored PSL; a hand-picked set of two-label suffixes for the countries covered.
**Decision.** `MULTI_LABEL_SUFFIXES` in `src/vigia/signals/urls.py` (com.co, gov.co, org.co, … co.uk) and otherwise the last two labels.
**Why.** 15 suffixes cover CO and US and the 47 cases; zero dependencies, zero network.
**What would invalidate it.** A new country pack with suffixes not on the list (add them), or false `brand_off_domain` on shared hosting (`*.github.io`, `*.blogspot.com`) → vendor the full PSL.
