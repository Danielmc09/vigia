# ADR-040 · Dependency-free campaign fingerprints: per-word simhash and a grid pHash (2026-10-27, Claude Code)

**Context.** specs/10 §4 asks for a 64-bit simhash over normalized text (no numbers or URLs) and image pHash, with Hamming distances <=6 and <=10. No simhash or imaging library is installed (Pillow/imagehash; D-024, D-033).
**Options.** `simhash`/`imagehash` + Pillow; hand-write both against the stdlib.
**Decision.** `domain/knowledge.py`. Text: `clean_text` -> drop tokens inside any URL found by `extract_urls` -> accent-fold and lowercase -> words matching `[a-z]{2,}` (drops numbers, punctuation, accents); one feature per word hashed with 8-byte `blake2b`, combined via Charikar's algorithm. Fewer than `MIN_WORDS = 8` words yields `None` (matches nothing). pHash: DCT-II over a 32x32 grayscale grid, the 8x8 lowest frequencies, bit = coefficient > median (excluding the DC term, which only measures brightness). Turning image bytes into the grid needs a decoder, deferred to T-12 with an `xfail(strict=True)` test (`image_phash`).
**Why.** ~40 lines, zero dependencies, deterministic; measured against a 24-word DIAN template, one changed word gives a 3-5 bit distance and two gives ~8, consistent with the spec's threshold of 6.
**What would invalidate it.** Legitimate-text collisions against the seed (`validate_cases` reports them), or a >1% collision false-positive rate (D-009) → 2-word shingles or a higher `MIN_WORDS`.
