# ADR-061 · `services/process_case.classify` is the only path, skipping the analyst when rules already decide (2026-10-27, Claude Code)

**Context.** The signals → `Evidence` → `build_verdict` wiring was duplicated 3 times (`app.py` /caso, `tools/measure.py:deterministic`, `grade_adversarial`; a deferral from reviewer T-08). With the analyst in place, calling it when a hard signal or 2 distinct medium signals already yield "scam" (specs/05 rules 1-2) only spends money: the analyst cannot lower that label.

**Decision.** `classify(case, pack, gate, analyst)` in `services/process_case.py`, used by all three callers; `verdict/fusion.py:decided_by_rules` (over `soften_citations`, same as `fuse`) decides whether to skip. A skipped case comes out `degraded=True` because `build_verdict` marks every verdict without an analyst as degraded: `medir.py`'s `degradados` counts those too (27 of 66 in the first fast run).

**Why.** One single pipeline, no divergence between endpoint, measurement and adversarial grading; less spend.

**What would invalidate it.** The panel or the video needing to distinguish "decided by rules" from "no model" (then a new field on `Verdict`, not reusing `degraded`).
