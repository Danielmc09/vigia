# ADR-097 · The team alert respects Meta's 24-hour window (2026-10-31, Claude Code)

**Context.** T-18b: Meta only allows free-text to someone who wrote in the last 24h; outside that, only an approved template. The alert was sent as text to the whole team and failed silently for anyone who hadn't written in.

**Options.** (a) always send a template — costs money, depends on approval, loses the long hook. (b) per-member routing by `members.last_inbound_at`: <24h → text (`POST /messages`); ≥24h → template `alerta_equipo_estafa` (`POST /templates`); no template configured → `Rejection TEMPLATE_PENDING` stored in `verdicts.alert_rejections` and visible on the panel; someone who never wrote gets nothing (D-087). (c) queue until the member writes — more state, late alert.

**Decision.** (b). `policy.actions.alert_route` decides (code, not model); `last_inbound_at` is touched on every WhatsApp message and on enrollment. Template params are built by `team_alert_params` from i18n, one line each.

**Deviation.** The spec says `reason=`; `Rejection` uses `error_code`. `store.team` filters by `business_id` in addition to RLS (defense in depth).

**What would invalidate it.** Meta changing the 24h rule, the template being rejected (everything out of window ends in `TEMPLATE_PENDING`), or needing to notify someone who never wrote.
