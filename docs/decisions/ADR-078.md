# ADR-078 · Images: longest side capped at 2048 px before paying Spark (2026-10-28, Claude Code)

**Context.** Judge T-12 (MINOR): `check_image` allows up to 25 Mpx/10 MB (D-077) and `SparkClient.read_image` sent the whole file as base64 (~13 MB body, uncapped input tokens).

**Decision.** `domain/image.py:shrink` leaves the image untouched if its longest side fits `READ_MAX_SIDE = 2048`, otherwise downsizes it (LANCZOS, aspect preserved): JPEG/WebP keep their format at quality 85, everything else becomes PNG; unsupported modes (CMYK, incident `adv-imagen-cmyk`) convert to RGB; `read_image` calls it before building the data URL. zxing and SAM still see the original: the QR is decoded at full resolution.

**Why.** A phone screenshot (~1170×2532) shrinks only ~20% and text stays legible; a 6000×4000 photo drops from 24 Mpx to 2.8 Mpx.

**What would invalidate it.** A real eval where a screenshot's text becomes unreadable after shrinking, or Spark already downsizing server-side making this a no-op.
