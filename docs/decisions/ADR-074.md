# ADR-074 · A `legitimo_*` category may expect `dudoso` if the case is ambiguous (2026-10-27, Claude Code)

**Context.** specs/06 said both "a non-null `ambiguo` may expect `dudoso`" and "`legitimo_*` requires `legitimo`"; the blind evaluator wrote c-0185 (a supplier catalog) and c-0186 (invoice reminder) as `legitimo_otro` + `dudoso`, and the validator rejected them.

**Decision.** The category describes who the sender is; `esperado` is what policy allows asserting. `legitimo_*` accepts `legitimo` or `dudoso` (the latter already requires `ambiguo`).

**Why.** Keeps the categories about identity, not about what the gate allows saying.

**What would invalidate it.** `por_categoria` needing to split legit cases the policy leaves doubtful (then a dedicated category).
