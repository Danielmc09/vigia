# ADR-095 · The team alert carries a signal hook and a disarmed indicator (2026-10-30, Claude Code)

**Context.** T-10d: the alert only said "X reported a SIMIT scam"; a coworker couldn't recognize it when the real thing arrived. Forwarding the original is ruled out (live link + reporter's data, D-017).

**Options.** (a) a model summary — violates rule 14. (b) hook = the catalog phrase (`signal.<type>`) of the strongest deterministic signal (`by_weight`); indicator = the `domain` from the strongest `urls`/`qr` evidence that isn't `official_domain` (otherwise the email sender's domain), with "." → "[.]", plus a phone from the text (with "+" or a 10-digit CO 3xx mobile) truncated by dropping the last 7 digits ("+57 300 ***").

**Decision.** (b). `channel/templates.team_alert_message(lang, reporter, verdict, case)`. The domain comes from signals, not from the first link in the text: a scam that also links the official site must not accuse it. Never a path, query, scheme, free text, or the forwarder's number. No indicator → no "disarmed" line or note. The webhook test's cap raised from 200 to 300 characters. The phone regex is not unified with the other three (telemetry masks any long number, commands accepts any format, the address book compares digits) because here account numbers must be excluded.

**What would invalidate it.** WhatsApp making `domain[.]tld` clickable on some client, or the 300-char cap truncating the alert in the phone notification.

**Adjustment after the 2nd review (Oct 30).** The indicator excludes any domain an `official_domain` signal marks official (`http_without_tls` carries it too), and the hook is never `official_domain` itself. With "+" the phone needs ≥10 digits to count.
