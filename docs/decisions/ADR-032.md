# ADR-032 · Country-pack patterns: accent-folded, whole word, `release` and `bank_account` categories, mild credential requests without a link (2026-10-27, Claude Code)

**Context.** specs/04 patterns are written with and without accents and users write without; asking for a "password" with no link also appears in legitimate messages ("never share your password"); a personal payment ("send to Nequi") alone is common in honest marketplace deals.
**Options.** Regex as-is with IGNORECASE; fold accents in text and pattern and require word boundaries.
**Decision.** NFD folding that preserves offsets (the evidence is the original snippet), `\b(?:pattern)\b`. `credentials_request` is hard only with a non-official link (otherwise mild); `personal_payment` is mild unless paired with `release` (held package/account) → hard `personal_payment_release`. Supplier book: an account is a number of >=6 digits and <=40 characters near a `bank_account` word (so a phone number doesn't count), and with a free email provider (gmail, outlook...) the sender must match exactly, not by domain.
**Why.** Without this, legitimate messages with "password" or a phone number become scams; recall loses c-0012 and c-0015 in exchange.
**What would invalidate it.** `fp_legitimo` staying at 0 with many doubtfuls once the analyst is added (T-09) → raise credentials to a medium weight.
